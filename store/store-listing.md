## Full description (max 4000 characters)
PersonalPocket is a secure, on-device vault for everything you'd normally
scatter across notes apps and sticky notes: logins, banking details,
investments, and loans.

Everything is encrypted on your phone with a key derived from your own PIN.
There is no server, no account, and no sync — your records never leave
your device.

KEY FEATURES
• Organize credentials, banking, investments, and loans in one place
• Unlock with a 6-digit PIN or fingerprint/Face ID
• AES encryption with PBKDF2 key derivation — industry-standard cryptography
• Auto-lock after 2 minutes of inactivity or when the app goes to background
• Screenshot blocking and an app-switcher privacy cover
• Encrypted export for your own backups, and a one-tap permanent wipe
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no internet permission required

WHY ON-DEVICE ONLY
Most password and finance managers ask you to trust a company's servers
with your most sensitive data. PersonalPocket doesn't — your encrypted
vault lives only on your phone, protected by the same secure hardware
(Keystore/Keychain) your device already uses to protect its lock screen.

PersonalPocket is built for people who want a private place to keep
financial information organized, without handing it to anyone else.
