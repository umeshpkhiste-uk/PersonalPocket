Privacy Policy for PersonalPocket

Last updated: October 3, 2026

PersonalPocket ("the app") is an on-device personal finance vault built to keep your credentials, banking, investment, and loan records private. This policy explains what happens to your data in plain terms.

Summary

  • PersonalPocket does not have a backend server for your data.
  • Your records never leave your device. Nothing is uploaded, synced, or transmitted to us, to banks, to brokers, or to any analytics or advertising service.
  • We do not collect, see, sell, or share your personal information, because we never receive it in the first place.

What data the app stores, and where

All records you add (credentials, banking details, investment and loan information) are encrypted on your device using a key derived from your PIN (PBKDF2 / AES, via industry-standard cryptography libraries) before they are written to storage. The encrypted data stays in the app's private storage area on your phone, and the decryption key material is held in your device's secure hardware-backed keystore (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). We, the developer, have no access to this key or to your records.

Biometric authentication

If you enable fingerprint or face unlock, PersonalPocket uses your operating system's built-in biometric authentication APIs to verify your identity locally on the device. Your biometric data is managed entirely by the operating system and is never accessible to the app or transmitted anywhere.

Exporting your data

You may choose to export an encrypted backup of your vault using your device's native share sheet. This action is entirely user-initiated: the app does not export, upload, or transmit your data on its own, and the exported file goes only to the destination you pick (e.g., your own cloud drive, email, or local storage).

Permissions the app requests, and why

  • Biometric / Face ID — to unlock your vault without typing your PIN.
  • File sharing — only when you explicitly choose to export a backup.
  • Screenshot / screen-recording block — the app blocks screenshots of your sensitive records as a privacy protection; this does not involve any data leaving the device.

Third parties

PersonalPocket does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs that transmit data off the device. The app includes no tracking of any kind.

Data deletion

You can permanently erase all vault data at any time from within the app ("Wipe data"), or by uninstalling the app, which removes all app-associated storage from your device per standard OS behavior.

Children's privacy

PersonalPocket is a personal finance tool intended for general audiences and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any information from children, because the app does not collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised and the new policy will be published at this same URL.
